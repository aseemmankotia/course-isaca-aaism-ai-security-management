# Chapter 1 Lab: AI Governance Foundations: Frameworks as Program Inputs

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Build a framework-mapping matrix assigning NIST AI RMF functions, ISO/IEC 42001 clauses, and MITRE ATLAS tactics to a sample AI security program charter for a fictional bank deploying a customer-facing chatbot.

## Key takeaway

Treat NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, MITRE ATLAS, and the EU AI Act as distinct inputs feeding one AI security program, not interchangeable substitutes for each other.

# Chapter 5 Lab: AI Threat and Vulnerability Management: Adversarial ML and ATLAS Mapping

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Map five described AI attack scenarios to the corresponding MITRE ATLAS tactic and OWASP LLM Top 10 category, then identify a defensive control for each, framed strictly as authorized defensive analysis.

## Key takeaway

Precisely distinguishing AI attack types and mapping each to MITRE ATLAS is essential for selecting the correct defensive control, and this distinction is heavily tested.

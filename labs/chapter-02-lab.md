# Chapter 2 Lab: AI Asset, Data Lifecycle, and Policy Management

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Draft an AI asset inventory template and classification tiering scheme for a fictional healthcare provider running three fine-tuned LLMs and two third-party foundation-model APIs.

## Key takeaway

AI asset lifecycle management must track provenance and lineage in addition to standard inventory and classification practices, and actively hunt for shadow AI.

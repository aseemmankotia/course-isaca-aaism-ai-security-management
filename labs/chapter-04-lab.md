# Chapter 4 Lab: AI Risk Assessment, Appetite, and Treatment

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Populate an AI risk register entry for a generative-AI code-assistant deployment, including likelihood, impact, treatment, and residual risk rating.

## Key takeaway

AI risk management requires dynamic, use-case-specific thresholds and treatment decisions because model risk profiles change over time, unlike many static IT risks.

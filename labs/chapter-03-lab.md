# Chapter 3 Lab: AI Security Program Management, Training, BCP, and Incident Response

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Write a one-page AI incident-response playbook covering a prompt-injection incident against a customer-service chatbot, including detection, escalation, and recovery steps.

## Key takeaway

AI security program management extends existing roles, training, metrics, and IR/BCP practices with AI-specific triage, accountability, and reporting rather than replacing them.

# Chapter 6 Lab: AI Vendor, Third-Party, and Supply-Chain Risk Management

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Evaluate a sample foundation-model vendor security addendum and data-usage agreement, flagging three clauses that fail an AI-specific due-diligence checklist.

## Key takeaway

AI vendor risk management requires AI-specific due diligence — provenance, data-usage terms, and model-card review — beyond standard third-party security assessments.
